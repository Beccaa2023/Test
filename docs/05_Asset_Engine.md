# Asset Engine

Version 4.0

---

# Purpose

The Asset Engine translates macroeconomic conditions into expected asset performance.

Its purpose is not to predict prices.

Its purpose is to estimate which asset classes are likely to outperform or underperform under the current macro regime.

The Asset Engine always starts from macro fundamentals.

Never from recent market performance.

---

# Asset Pricing Framework

Every asset is ultimately driven by five macro forces.

Growth

Inflation

Liquidity

Credit

Valuation

Different assets have different sensitivities.

The task of the Asset Engine is to identify those sensitivities.

# Relative Strength

MOS compares assets rather than predicting absolute returns.

Instead of asking

Will Technology rise?

Ask

Will Technology outperform Utilities?

Relative performance is more robust than absolute forecasts.
