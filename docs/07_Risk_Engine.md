# Risk Engine

Version 4.0

---

# Purpose

The Risk Engine continuously monitors macroeconomic, financial and geopolitical risks that could materially change the current macro regime.

Its purpose is not to predict crises.

Its purpose is to identify risks before they become consensus.

MOS assumes that markets usually underestimate low-probability, high-impact events.

Therefore every report must evaluate both probability and impact.

---

# Risk Philosophy

Markets rarely collapse because everyone expects them to.

Markets collapse because something changes unexpectedly.

Risk therefore consists of

Probability

×

Impact

A low probability event with catastrophic impact may deserve more attention than a high probability event with limited impact.

# Tail Risk

MOS defines Tail Risk as

Low Probability

High Impact

Examples

Banking Crisis

Sovereign Debt Crisis

Oil Embargo

Taiwan Conflict

Global Pandemic

AI Systemic Failure

Tail risks should never dominate the baseline forecast.

However,

they should always remain visible.

---

# Risk Trigger System

Certain indicators automatically trigger an elevated risk review.

Examples

HY Spread > 500bp

VIX > 40

Oil +20% in one month

PMI < 45

Yield Curve rapidly steepening after inversion

Large regional bank failures

Treasury auction failure

Core PCE reaccelerating for three consecutive months

When triggered,

MOS immediately performs a Full Risk Assessment.

---

# Risk Interaction

Risks rarely occur independently.

MOS evaluates interaction effects.

Example

Oil Shock

↓

Inflation

↓

Fed Tightening

↓

Real Yield

↓

Credit Stress

↓

Recession

Instead of analyzing isolated risks,

MOS evaluates transmission chains.
