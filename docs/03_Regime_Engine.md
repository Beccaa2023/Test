# Regime Engine

Version 4.0

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Regime Engine is not to predict recessions.

Its purpose is to estimate the current macroeconomic regime and monitor regime transitions.

Markets move because expectations change.

Expectations change because the macro regime changes.

Therefore identifying regime transitions is more important than identifying recessions.

---

# Core Question

Always answer:

Where are we?

Where are we going?

How confident are we?

---

# The Five-State Model

Instead of classifying the economy into only four quadrants, MOS expands the framework into five regimes.

State 1

Expansion

Characteristics

Growth accelerating

Inflation stable

Employment improving

Credit expanding

Liquidity neutral

Typical Assets

Equities ↑

Credit ↑

Small Caps ↑

Dollar →

Treasuries ↓

---

State 2

Goldilocks

Characteristics

Growth stable

Inflation falling

Fed easing expectations

Financial conditions improving

Typical Assets

Technology ↑

Long-duration assets ↑

Treasuries ↑

Credit ↑

Gold →

---

State 3

Reflation

Characteristics

Growth accelerating

Inflation accelerating

Commodity demand rising

Fed becoming hawkish

Typical Assets

Energy ↑

Industrials ↑

Banks ↑

Oil ↑

Gold ↑

Long Bonds ↓

---

State 4

Stagflation

Characteristics

Growth slowing

Inflation sticky

Real income falling

Policy constrained

Typical Assets

Gold ↑

Oil ↑

Defensive Stocks ↑

Treasuries ↓

Growth Stocks ↓

---

State 5

Recession

Characteristics

Growth contracting

Inflation falling

Employment weakening

Credit deteriorating

Fed easing aggressively

Typical Assets

Treasuries ↑

Utilities ↑

Healthcare ↑

Gold →

High Yield ↓

Small Caps ↓
