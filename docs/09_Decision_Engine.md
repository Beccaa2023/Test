# Decision Engine

Version 4.0

---

# Purpose

The Decision Engine integrates all previous MOS modules into a coherent macro investment conclusion.

Its purpose is not to maximize returns.

Its purpose is to make consistent, evidence-based and repeatable decisions.

Every investment conclusion must originate from the macro framework rather than market sentiment.

---

# Decision Philosophy

MOS does not make decisions based on headlines.

MOS does not make decisions based on emotions.

MOS does not make decisions based on short-term market movements.

MOS makes decisions based on

Evidence

↓

Probability

↓

Risk

↓

Asset Allocation

↓

Positioning

# Decision Principles

Principle 1

Facts before opinions.

Every conclusion must reference measurable evidence.

---

Principle 2

Probability before certainty.

Express scenarios as probabilities.

Never use absolute language.

---

Principle 3

Risk before return.

Estimate downside before upside.

Protect capital first.

---

Principle 4

Consistency before complexity.

Follow the framework even when market narratives change.

# Tactical View

Time Horizon

1–12 Weeks

Questions

Are tactical adjustments justified?

Tactical positioning must not contradict the strategic framework without strong evidence.

# Portfolio Discipline

MOS emphasizes diversification across macro drivers.

Avoid concentrating exposure to a single macro outcome.

Maintain flexibility when probabilities change.

Reduce exposure when confidence declines.

Increase exposure only when evidence strengthens.

---

# Decision Review

Every decision should be evaluated later.

Questions

Was probability assessment reasonable?

Was risk underestimated?

Was discipline maintained?

# Investment Committee Format

The final output should fit onto one page and answer the following questions.

1.

What changed?

2.

Why did it change?

3.

What is the highest probability scenario?

4.

What are the biggest risks?

5.

How should the portfolio change?

6.

What data could invalidate today's view?

Decision quality is judged by the quality of reasoning rather than short-term market outcomes.

# Final Principle

A good decision is not one that always produces profits.

A good decision is one that is

Evidence-based

Probabilistic

Risk-aware

Repeatable

Continuously reviewed

MOS seeks disciplined decision making under uncertainty rather than certainty about the future.
