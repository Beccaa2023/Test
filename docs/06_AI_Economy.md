# AI Economy Engine

Version 4.0

---

# Purpose

The AI Economy Engine evaluates whether artificial intelligence is becoming a macroeconomic force.

MOS does not treat AI as a technology sector.

MOS treats AI as a structural driver that may permanently change productivity, labor markets, corporate profitability, inflation dynamics and capital allocation.

The purpose of this Engine is to monitor these structural changes.

---

# Philosophy

Previous industrial revolutions increased output by replacing physical labor.

Artificial intelligence increases output by augmenting or replacing cognitive labor.

Therefore AI affects nearly every macro variable.

Growth

Employment

Inflation

Productivity

Corporate Margins

Capital Expenditure

Labor Allocation

MOS evaluates AI through macro evidence rather than narratives.
