# AI Economy Handbook

## Purpose

The AI Economy Handbook explains how MOS evaluates artificial intelligence as a structural macroeconomic driver.

AI is not treated as a technology sector.

MOS treats AI as a long-term force capable of reshaping productivity, labor markets, capital investment and economic growth.

Unlike cyclical indicators, AI primarily influences the long-run supply side of the economy.

# Core Indicator 1

## Labor Productivity

Importance

★★★★★

Classification

Structural

---

### Why It Matters

Productivity measures the amount of output generated per unit of labor.

Sustained productivity growth allows the economy to expand without generating excessive inflation.

MOS considers productivity the primary channel through which AI influences the macroeconomy.

---

### Interpretation

Productivity Rising

↓

Potential GDP increases

↓

Inflation pressure may ease

↓

Real income improves

↓

Long-term economic growth strengthens

# Core Indicator 2

## Total Factor Productivity (TFP)

Importance

★★★★★

Classification

Structural

---

### Why It Matters

TFP measures improvements in economic efficiency beyond increases in labor and capital.

Technological innovation has historically been one of the most important drivers of TFP growth.

MOS uses TFP as a long-term measure of structural economic progress.

# Core Indicator 6

## Potential GDP Growth

Importance

★★★★★

Classification

Structural

---

Potential GDP represents the maximum sustainable level of economic output without generating excessive inflation.

Persistent productivity gains from AI may raise potential GDP over time.

MOS views changes in potential growth as one of the most important long-term consequences of AI adoption.

# Common Analytical Mistakes

Mistake 1

Treating AI as only a technology sector.

Correction:

Evaluate AI as a structural driver affecting the entire economy.

---

Mistake 2

Assuming technological breakthroughs immediately increase productivity.

Correction:

Adoption and organizational change usually take time.

---

Mistake 3

Focusing only on hardware investment.

Correction:

Monitor software integration, workforce adaptation and business processes.

---

Mistake 4

Ignoring labor market adjustments.

Correction:

Evaluate both productivity gains and changes in employment composition.

# Key Questions

Every AI Economy analysis should answer:

Is AI improving productivity?

Are businesses adopting AI at scale?

Is AI increasing potential economic growth?

How is AI changing labor demand?

What are the long-term implications for inflation, monetary policy and asset allocation?

---

# Final Principle

AI should not be analysed as a short-term market theme.

MOS evaluates AI as a structural force capable of reshaping productivity, economic growth and the long-term macroeconomic environment.

Its effects emerge gradually through investment, adoption and productivity rather than through individual market events.
