# How to Read Macroeconomic Indicators

Version 4.0

---

# Purpose

Macroeconomic analysis is not the collection of statistics.

It is the interpretation of economic signals.

This guide explains how MOS reads every economic indicator.

The purpose is to establish a consistent analytical method before studying individual indicators.

---

# Principle 1

One number means almost nothing.

Never draw macro conclusions from a single release.

Every indicator should be interpreted together with

Previous

Trend

Consensus

Revision

Related Indicators

Example

Payroll

220k

Looks strong.

But

Previous revised

260k → 110k

Labor market is actually weakening.

MOS never evaluates only headlines.

---

# Principle 2

Direction is more important than level.

Markets care about change.

Not absolute values.

Example

Inflation

3%

↓

2.7%

↓

2.4%

↓

2.2%

Even though inflation is still above target,

the trend is improving.

Trend usually matters more than level.

---

# Principle 3

Momentum matters.

MOS always asks

Accelerating?

Decelerating?

Stable?

Turning Point?

Instead of

High?

Low?

Turning points create market opportunities.

---

# Principle 4

Surprises move markets.

Markets compare

Actual

vs

Consensus

Not

Actual

vs

Last Month

Example

Expected

Payroll

120k

Actual

180k

Bullish surprise.

Example

Expected

250k

Actual

180k

Bearish surprise.

The same data.

Different market reaction.

---

# Principle 5

Leading indicators deserve greater attention.

Leading

↓

Coincident

↓

Lagging

Never reverse this order.

---

# Principle 6

Cross confirmation.

One indicator

↓

Hypothesis

Three independent indicators

↓

Evidence

Five indicators

↓

High Confidence

---

# Principle 7

Separate signal from noise.

Economic data contain noise.

MOS evaluates

One Month

Three Months

Six Months

Annual Trend

Never overreact to one release.

---

# Principle 8

Markets price expectations.

Economic data

↓

Expectations

↓

Market Pricing

MOS always asks

Has the market already priced this?

---

# Principle 9

Interpret the transmission chain.

Every indicator ultimately affects

Growth

↓

Inflation

↓

Fed

↓

Treasury

↓

Liquidity

↓

Credit

↓

Corporate Earnings

↓

Asset Prices

Never jump directly from data to stock prices.

---

# Principle 10

Every indicator has limitations.

GDP

Lagging.

Payroll

Often revised.

PMI

Survey based.

Consumer Confidence

Sentiment.

Housing

Interest-rate sensitive.

MOS evaluates strengths and weaknesses.

---

# Final Principle

Indicators do not predict markets.

Indicators improve probabilities.

MOS does not seek certainty.

MOS seeks better decisions.
